Crelix AI is not a model. We don't train our own LLM. Instead, we're an execution layer that sits between frontier AI models and running systems. At the bottom, we orchestrate the best models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. In the middle, the Crelix Execution Layer coordinates specialized agents — planner, designer, engineer, tester, deployer, security — as one team. At the top, running systems: deployed pages, apps, and scans that you can actually use. This is what sets us apart. We're not another chatbot wrapper. We're the infrastructure that turns AI intelligence into real business outcomes.
