The workflow is four steps. First, describe your project — what you're building, who it's for, the angle. Second, add references — screenshots, brand colors, Figma links. The agents match your vision. Third, pair launch and security — create the page, iterate on copy and visuals, then run a pre-launch scan without leaving the EKAM AI workflow. Fourth, go live with one click — publish, redeploy edits, and hand the live URL to Pentest. Hosting is included. The entire process takes an afternoon, not a sprint, and every step happens inside one cohesive platform.
