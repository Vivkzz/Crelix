Pentest is our second suite. Before you announce your launch, you run a pre-launch security scan. We spawn 12 specialist agents — one per vulnerability class — in parallel. They cover the OWASP Top 10 plus business logic errors, race conditions, and SSRF. Every finding comes with request/response evidence, severity rating, and remediation guidance. It's authorized targets only, and aligned with SOC 2 and GDPR controls. This is not a replacement for a full audit, but it catches the obvious issues before you drive traffic.
